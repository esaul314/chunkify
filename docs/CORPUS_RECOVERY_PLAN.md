# Explicit source recovery

Recover unsupported extraction only through opt-in adapter settings. Retain the
original files, text order, source locations and explicit errors. Native OCR may
fill genuinely text-empty PDF pages; declared-body EPUB recovery may ignore absent
nontext assets while refusing missing or encrypted body documents.

Validate generated positive and negative fixtures, unchanged defaults, complete
text coverage and provenance through the existing supported-Python quality gates.
Reader success is separate from downstream enrichment and retrieval acceptance.
Keep actual source controls and operational receipts outside tracked source.
